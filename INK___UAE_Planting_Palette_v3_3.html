Foliage palette

Suggested palette combinations

Arid native tapestry
Naturalistic open landscape · wadi fringe · linear parks
Prosopis cineraria→ Acacia tortilis→ Atriplex halimus→ Leucophyllum frutescens→ Cenchrus ciliaris→ Tephrosia apollinea

Sandy buff and grey-green tones. Textural contrast between feathery grasses and hard shrub forms. Zero supplemental irrigation once established. Authentic Gulf desert ecology with high biodiversity value.

Native acacia woodland
Transitional zone · buffer planting · large scale parks
Acacia tortilis→ Ziziphus spina-christi→ Salvadora persica→ Atriplex nummularia→ Aerva javanica→ Sporobolus spicatus

Multi-species acacia canopy with Ziziphus mid-layer, Salvadora shrub base, Aerva cotton groundcover. Replicates natural wadi fringe ecology. Minimal maintenance once established.

Desert grass meadow
Open parkway · dune stabilisation · erosion slopes
Panicum turgidum→ Cenchrus ciliaris→ Stipagrostis plumosa→ Tephrosia apollinea→ Zygophyllum coccineum

Warm ochre and bronze grasses punctuated by delicate wildflower drifts. Seasonal interest, light and airy in character. Dune-binding root systems.

Suggested palette combinations

Vibrant Mediterranean — formal entry
Entry plaza · streetscape · exposed open parkway
Olea europaea→ Cupressus sempervirens→ Rosmarinus officinalis→ Lavandula officinalis→ Leucophyllum frutescens

Silvery olive canopy, dark cypress verticals, purple-silver rosemary and lavender mass. Cool-toned, aromatic, architectural. Extremely low TSE demand.

Bold Mediterranean accent
Entry boulevard · landmark feature · formal axis
Olea europaea→ Bougainvillea spectabilis→ Nerium oleander→ Gazania rigens→ Pennisetum setaceum

Gnarled olive canopy with vivid Bougainvillea cascades, oleander mass planting, Gazania foreground, and fountain grass texture.

Aromatic herb garden
Wellness garden · outdoor dining · sensory path
Vitex agnus-castus→ Myrtus communis→ Rosmarinus officinalis→ Lavandula officinalis→ Santolina chamaecyparissus

All aromatic species. Purple-flowered Vitex canopy, clipped myrtle structure, rosemary and lavender drifts, silver santolina edging. Sensory-led. Works for wellness, spa, and hospitality projects.

"Deep glossy greens, bold structural palms, and vivid seasonal flowering. Lush and theatrical — designed for shaded courtyards, irrigated parkways, and landmark boulevard planting."

Suggested palette combinations

Shaded tropical oasis
Shaded courtyard · atrium · private garden
Ficus benjamina→ Heliconia psittacorum→ Strelitzia reginae→ Agapanthus africanus→ Rhoeo spathacea

Deep glossy canopy, exotic bracts and bold sculptural accent shrubs. Deploy in shaded microclimates to reduce TSE demand.

Tropical boulevard — full sun
Primary avenue · grand axis · kerb bed zones
Delonix regia→ Peltophorum pterocarpum→ Bougainvillea spectabilis→ Adenium obesum→ Lantana camara

Flamboyant canopy with Bougainvillea accent masses and Adenium as base feature shrubs. Seasonal colour. High visual impact for landmark boulevards.

Palm court
Formal plaza · resort arrival · courtyard paving
Phoenix dactylifera→ Roystonea regia→ Dypsis lutescens→ Strelitzia reginae→ Agapanthus africanus

Layered palm canopy from tall date and royal palms down to golden cane clusters. Bird of paradise and agapanthus form the ground layer.
